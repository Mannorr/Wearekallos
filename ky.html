The Concept

Style meets
purpose.

Kiwi & Yam was founded with a vision of challenging conventions in fashion while creating a space for cultural storytelling and self-expression. Two ingredients from two continents — kiwi from the West, yam rooted deep in Africa — fused into one expressive brand.

The brand represents a generation that is bold, unfiltered, and socially conscious. By merging activism with design, Kiwi & Yam builds a movement — one that speaks to the rebels, the creatives, and those who shape the culture of tomorrow.

Every visual decision — the logo, the colour palette, the iconography — is an act of cultural assertion. This brand wears its heritage loudly and its ambitions even louder.

Unified Mark

The logo is a fusion of K and Y into one single unified mark. Its organic curves draw inspiration from traditional African motifs — giving the brand a sense of heritage and authenticity while its bold form ensures instant recognition across every surface.

Logo System

Six marks.
One movement.

The logo system lives across two formats — a horizontal lockup pairing the mark with the wordmark, and a stacked centred lockup. Both are deployed across five distinct colourways. The mark is always the same — only the context changes.

Mint is the brand's primary energy — fresh, alive, and distinctly Kiwi & Yam. It signals the brand's Nigerian roots filtered through a modern lens: vibrant, confident, impossible to ignore. Purple adds depth and creative tension — the colour of ambition, of royalty reimagined.

Wine grounds the palette in heritage. It is a nod to depth, to history, to the weight of culture. Sand balances the heat — the neutral backdrop against which everything else pops. Ink anchors it all with intention and authority.
